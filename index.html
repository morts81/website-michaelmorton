Elearning & instructional design

Elearning design for the global eye health and disability sector

I help NGOs, foundations, and health organisations turn clinical and technical expertise into training that actually changes practice - from refraction curricula to advocacy courses to accessibility training.

Contact me →

The problem I solve

Sector expertise (global eye health, optometry, dispensing, and disability services) is often locked in the heads of specialists, or scattered across outdated content. I turn that into structured, engaging learning that works for frontline staff, volunteers, and global teams - including in low-resource and multilingual contexts.

Selected work

  1. 01

    SEE Together

    Refraction and dispensing curriculum

    Challenge
    Core refraction and dispensing skills sat with a small number of practitioners, with no consistent way to train new staff at scale.
    What I built
    A structured curriculum covering refraction and dispensing, sequenced for practical application and delivered online.
    Outcome
    New staff across partner sites now train to a single standard, cutting onboarding time and reducing reliance on senior clinicians to teach the basics.
  2. 02

    ZEISS

    Myopia Education Program

    Challenge
    Eye care professionals needed practical, evidence-based guidance on managing myopia in children, a fast-moving area where clinical practice varies widely.
    What I built
    Modular online learning for eye care professionals covering myopia science, risk assessment, management options and conversations with parents.
    Outcome
    A self-paced program practitioners can complete around clinic hours, giving ZEISS a consistent education offer across markets.
  3. 03

    The Fred Hollows Foundation

    Advocacy training course and chatbot

    Challenge
    Teams across multiple countries needed to advocate effectively for eye health, but guidance was long-form and rarely used in the moment.
    What I built
    An advocacy course paired with a chatbot that answers questions and rehearses scenarios after the course ends.
    Outcome
    Country teams have a shared advocacy framework and an on-demand tool to prepare for meetings with government and partners.
  4. 04

    Guide Dogs

    Technology job aid

    Challenge
    Staff supporting clients with assistive technology needed answers at the point of use, not a course to sit through.
    What I built
    A searchable job aid designed for quick reference in the field, accessible on the devices staff already carry.
    Outcome
    Staff find answers in seconds during client sessions, with fewer escalations to specialist technology advisers.

About

I'm an elearning developer and instructional designer. I take projects from concept stage through to evaluation, and I'm committed to design that elevates performance rather than just looking good.

Working in eye health and disability has shaped how I design: courses should be accessible and engaging for everyone, including those learning in a second language, for those with vision loss or other disabilities, and for teams delivering care where resources are thin.
